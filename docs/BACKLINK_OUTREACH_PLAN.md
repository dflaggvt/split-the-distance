Earn relevant backlinks to Split The Distance from pages where a halfway point calculator is genuinely useful: travel planning, road trips, long-distance relationships, family coordination, local guides, college groups, coworking communities, and meetup resources.

## Best prospect types

1. Travel and road trip blogs with resource pages.
2. Long-distance relationship blogs and forums with planning guides.
3. Parenting, co-parenting, and family meetup resources.
4. Local tourism sites that publish planning tools.
5. College alumni chapters, student organizations, and regional clubs.
6. Coworking, networking, and meetup communities.
7. Productivity and planning blogs with tool roundups.
8. Tool directories that list travel, map, or planning calculators.

## Outreach rules

- Prioritize pages that already recommend tools or resources.
- Ask for one natural link, not several.
- Point to the most relevant URL for their audience.
- Avoid paid link schemes, private blog networks, comment spam, and irrelevant directories.
- Track every contact, date, URL, and response.
- Follow up once after 5 to 7 business days.
